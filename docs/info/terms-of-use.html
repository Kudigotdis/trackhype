3. Voting rules and chart mechanics

All user voting and chart generation is governed by standard platform rules:

  • Ballot schedule: weekly voting windows close strictly every Monday at 00:00 (Africa/Harare time).

  • Ballot revisions: users may edit their active ballot throughout the chart week; only the final saved ballot at closing time is counted.
  • Anti-fraud: automated voting, scripts, bot accounts, or artificial engagement manipulation are prohibited and result in immediate ballot voiding and account suspension.
