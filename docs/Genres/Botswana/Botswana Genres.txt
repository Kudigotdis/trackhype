Key pioneers and mainstream artists include Zeus, Dramaboi, and HT Tautona.Kwaito Kwasa: A unique, homegrown fusion genre created in Botswana by mixing fast-paced South African township Kwaito with the hip-swaying rhythms of Congolese Kwasa Kwasa (Rhumba). It was popularized by the legendary Vee Mampeezy.Borankana (Traditional Music): Highly rhythmic folklore music that relies heavily on acoustic guitars, clappings, and intense foot-stamping dance routines. Mainstream cultural troupes like Culture Spears, Matsieng, and Machesa have turned this into a massive commercial genre.Kwasa Kwasa (Rhumba): Localized African rumba originating from Central Africa, which remains incredibly popular for its intricate guitar compositions and energetic dance moves. Traditional, Folklore & Sacred Genres
1. Borankana (Traditional Music)
Culture Spears – Iconic group that commercialized traditional Setswana dance and music across Southern Africa.

Matsieng – Famous for raw, high-octane Borankana storytelling and traditional foot-stamping dance routines.

Machesa Traditional Group – Award-winning pioneer troupe known for rich acoustic guitar work and traditional vocal arrangements.

Ditiro Leero – Master songwriter and former lead singer of Matsieng who defined modern folklore.

Thero – Prominent traditional performer preserving authentic Setswana dance and folklore rhythms.

2. Setapa
Shumba Ratshega – Legendary performer known for bringing fast-paced Setapa rhythms from the Kweneng region into mainstream pop.

Gong Master – The late "Ketshwerewe," a monumental voice in Setapa and commercial Setswana traditional music.

Kgwanyape Band – Early pioneers who brought traditional Setapa rhythms to electric stage setups.

Motsididi – Traditional troupe renowned for intricate footwork and authentic regional Setapa arrangements.

Moratiwa Traditional Group – Preservation ensemble showcasing classic Setapa harvest and social celebration routines.

3. Phathisi
Ngwao Letshwao Traditional Group – Acclaimed ensemble specializing in authentic Kweneng-style Phathisi dance and drumming.

Tsoga Africa Cultural Group – High-energy group preserving traditional leg-rattle (mathlo) percussion and Phathisi routines.

Molepolole Cultural Troupe – Regional group dedicated to original Phathisi ritual dance.

Kweneng Arts Collective – Performance troupe keeping historical Phathisi heritage alive at national festivals.

Sedibeng Traditional Group – Folk troupe known for signature Phathisi physical choreography and call-and-response vocals.

4. Tsutsube (San / Basarwa Rhythms)
Kalahari Jaos – Group dedicated to preserving authentic Basarwa trance rhythms and vocal styles.

Gantsi Craft Ensemble – Cultural collective performing traditional Tsutsube dance and music from Ghanzi.

D’Kar Cultural Troupe – Indigenous performers showcasing ancestral Basarwa healing and social dances.

RasTsekele – Artist who incorporates Tsutsube acoustic arrangements into broader folk music.

Qani Traditional Group – Ghanzi-based cultural group focused on preserving indigenous Kalahari trance-dance percussion.

5. Seperu
Veekuhane Cultural Group – Premier troupe from the Chobe region preserving UNESCO-inscribed Seperu dance and song.

Chobe Ensembles – Regional performers showcasing authentic Seperu dress, movement, and vocal dynamics.

Kasane Heritage Troupe – Preservation group focused on Northern Botswana riverine folklore.

Kavango-Chobe Cultural Collective – Performers dedicated to regional Seperu ritual dance.

Linyanti Traditional Group – Cultural troupe maintaining original Subiya/Veekuhane Seperu routines.

6. Hosana / Hosanna (Rainmaking Rhythms)
Ikalanga Jazz Band – Incorporates sacred Kalanga Hosana rainmaking rhythms into contemporary setups.

Mapoka Cultural Ensemble – Traditional group dedicated to authentic Kalanga spiritual and Hosana drumming.

Nswazwi Cultural Group – Preservation troupe performing traditional Hosana ceremonial songs and dances.

Tjilenje Tja Nswazwi – Award-winning Kalanga ensemble performing authentic Hosana percussion.

Bokalaka Heritage Troupe – Group focused on preserving North-Eastern Botswana rainmaking traditions.

7. Ndazula
Bukalanga Cultural Group – Performers dedicated to authentic Kalanga Ndazula social and ceremonial dance.

Domboshaba Cultural Ensemble – Troupe that highlights Ndazula performance at the annual Domboshaba Festival.

Sebina Traditional Arts – Group showcasing fast-paced Kalanga Ndazula drum routines.

Zwenshambe Cultural Group – Regional performers keeping heritage Ndazula choreography alive.

Nshakazhogwe Traditional Troupe – Community group specializing in Kalanga social dance forms.

8. Polka (Kgalagadi German-influenced Folk)
Khawa Polka Group – Award-winning group from the Kgalagadi region specializing in southwestern Botswana Polka.

Tsabong Polka Dancers – Renowned cultural group performing classic Kgalagadi Polka accordion and footwork routines.

Ghanzi Polka Troupe – Regional ensemble preserving historic Kgalagadi Polka performance styles.

Kuis Community Polka Group – Heritage performers showcasing authentic southern border Polka traditions.
