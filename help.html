Help, Rules & Policies

Everything about voting, charting, the hub and how TrackHype handles your data.

Voting Rules

How voting works
  • Tap Vote on any song, then enter the target position you want it to reach (1 to the chart size).
  • Your chosen song moves to that position and the incumbent shifts down one.
  • Points follow the chart: position #1 gives 20 points, #20 gives 1 point.
  • You get one vote per chart per 24 hours. The chart card shows when your next vote becomes available.
  • Voting is a Zimbabwean audience activity — TrackHype builds Zimbabwe's official listening and fan-vote charts.
Chart rules
  • Charts update through aggregation — a single fan vote never changes the published chart on its own.
  • Songs must be approved and chart eligible to appear on official charts.
  • Any attempt to manipulate rankings with automated or repeated votes is removed.
Payment does not guarantee charting

Artist submission fees cover processing, review and chart eligibility. They are not votes and do not buy a chart position.

FAQ

Which countries use TrackHype?

TrackHype is made for Zimbabwe. Charts, new music, artists and votes all focus on Zimbabwean music and audiences.

How much does it cost to submit music?

Artists pay $10.00 USD per song for submission, review and chart eligibility.

Can I listen to a song on TrackHype?

The song page plays a local sample video for demos. In production TrackHype plays official YouTube embeds and never downloads or hosts YouTube audio.

Where can I listen to the music?

Each song links out to its streaming profiles — Spotify, TikTok, YouTube, Apple Music (iTunes) and Amazon Music.

How do I follow artists and save songs?

Tap Follow on any artist page and heart on any song or chart entry. Favourites are saved on this device via your TrackHype profile.

Privacy

Your data

TrackHype stores the details you provide — name, email, mobile number, network, music preferences and favourites — to personalise your experience, send chart verdicts and reminders, and process artist submissions.

What we do not do

TrackHype does not sell your personal data, download or host YouTube audio, or share your payment details with third parties beyond the payment provider used to process your submission.

Terms of Use

Acceptable use

TrackHype is a discovery, charting and fan-voting space for Zimbabwean music. Users must be honest: one vote per chart per 24 hours, no bots, no vote manipulation and no misleading submissions.

Artist submissions

By submitting music you confirm you own or control the rights to the tracks, artwork and lyrics, and that the details you provide are accurate. TrackHype may reject any submission that does not meet its review standards.

Support

Get in touch

For artist submissions, chart questions, payments and general support, use the Help flow in the app. TrackHype support covers Zimbabwe and responds within a limited time window.
