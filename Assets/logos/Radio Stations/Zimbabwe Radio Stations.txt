Zimbabwe's radio stations, categorized by network type along with key details for each station:

### National Public Stations (ZBC)

The state-backed public broadcasting network operates four primary stations:

* **Radio Zimbabwe:** Focuses primarily on local languages, Zimbabwean culture, and community news.
* **Power FM:** The national urban contemporary station targeted at youth and music broadcasting.
* **National FM:** Dedicated entirely to minority languages and ethnic cultural programming.
* **Classic 263:** (Formerly Spot FM/SFM) Focuses on talk radio, current affairs, and classic music.

---

### National Commercial Stations

Nationwide, privately owned commercial stations:

* **Star FM (89.7 MHz):** Zimbabwe's first commercial station, owned by Zimpapers.
* **ZiFM Stereo (106.1 MHz):** The country's first privately owned urban music and talk station.

---

### Regional & Provincial Commercial Stations

Commercial stations serving specific cities, towns, and regions:

* **Capitalk 100.4 FM (Harare – 100.4 MHz):** Capital city talk radio, news, and current affairs.
* **Skyz Metro FM (Bulawayo – 100.3 MHz):** Focuses on Bulawayo urban culture and music.
* **Khulumani FM (Bulawayo – 95.0 MHz):** Targets the broader Bulawayo metropolitan community.
* **Diamond FM (Mutare – 103.8 MHz):** Provides regional coverage for the Manicaland province.
* **98.4 Midlands (Gweru – 98.4 MHz):** Focuses on youth, contemporary music, and local entertainment.
* **95.8 Central Radio (Gweru – 95.8 MHz):** Commercial talk and local news for the Midlands area.
* **Breeze FM (Victoria Falls – 91.2 MHz):** Covers the Victoria Falls tourism hub and local community news.
* **YAFM (Zvishavane – 91.8 MHz):** Tailored around the mining community, local news, and music.
* **Hevoi FM (Masvingo – 100.2 MHz):** Delivers Masvingo provincial news and regional programming.
* **Nyaminyami FM (Kariba – 94.5 MHz):** Serves the Zambezi Valley community with local content.
