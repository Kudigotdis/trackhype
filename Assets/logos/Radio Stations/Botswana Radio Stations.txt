## State-Owned Broadcasters (National)

### 1. Radio Botswana 1 (RB1)

Focuses on national heritage, agricultural programming, national news, and traditional folk rhythms (such as Setapa, Borankana, and Dikwaere).

* **Chart 1: RB1 Dipina le Maboko Top 10**
* **Sponsor:** Department of Broadcasting Services (DBS) / Local Agri-sponsors
* **Number of Songs:** 10
* **Day of Show:** Sunday afternoons
* **Presenter(s):** Thuso Letlhoma / Mogatusi "Kgosiemang" Kwapa
* **Information:** Highlights classic and modern Tswana folk music, traditional dance troupes, and spoken word poetry, preserving indigenous languages and traditional arrangements.


* **Chart 2: Radio Botswana Gospel Top 15**
* **Sponsor:** Botswana Christian Council / Corporate Partners
* **Number of Songs:** 15
* **Day of Show:** Sunday mornings
* **Presenter(s):** Masego Ditshwane
* **Information:** Tracks nationwide releases in Tswana and English gospel, focusing on church choirs, localized praise music, and spiritual solos.


* **Chart 3: RB1 Mmino wa Setso Monthly Top 20**
* **Sponsor:** Ministry of Youth, Gender, Sport and Culture
* **Number of Songs:** 20
* **Day of Show:** Last Saturday of every month
* **Presenter(s):** Segametsi Bome
* **Information:** A monthly index evaluating traditional folk, Ndazola, and Kalanga cultural hits submitted from district cultural centers across Botswana.



---

### 2. Radio Botswana 2 (RB2)

The commercial youth wing of state broadcasting, focusing on commercial pop, Amapiano, Afro-house, Hip Hop, and local urban tracks.

* **Chart 1: RB2 Top 40 Countdown**
* **Sponsor:** Mascom Wireless
* **Number of Songs:** 40
* **Day of Show:** Saturday mid-mornings to afternoon
* **Presenter(s):** DJ Sly (Unbathed/Sylvester) / Tefo "T-Bags" Phatshwane
* **Information:** RB2's flagship national weekly chart, tracking heavy-rotation singles, streaming trends, and listener SMS votes across Botswana and the SADC region.


* **Chart 2: The Local Vibe Top 10**
* **Sponsor:** Botswana Telecommunications Corporation (BTC)
* **Number of Songs:** 10
* **Day of Show:** Friday evenings
* **Presenter(s):** DJ Jazzy Dee
* **Information:** A localized urban countdown dedicated 100% to home-grown Botswana artists, spanning Motswako Hip Hop, local Amapiano, and Afro-pop bangers.


* **Chart 3: RB2 End of Year Top 100 Countdown**
* **Sponsor:** Orange Botswana / Mascom
* **Number of Songs:** 100
* **Day of Show:** December 31st (Annual marathon broadcast)
* **Presenter(s):** Full RB2 On-Air Crew
* **Information:** The state commercial station's largest annual show, determining the official "Song of the Year" across regional, global, and local categories based on year-long listener voting.

## Private Commercial & Campus Stations

### 3. Yarona FM (106.6 FM)

Gaborone’s leading urban youth station, heavily centered on Motswako, Hip-Hop, Amapiano, and pop chart-toppers.

* **Chart 1: Yarona FM Top 40**
* **Sponsor:** Orange Botswana
* **Number of Songs:** 40
* **Day of Show:** Saturday afternoons (12:00 PM – 3:00 PM)
* **Presenter(s):** King Bee / Ross L
* **Information:** The premier commercial youth chart in Gaborone, measuring airplay, request statistics, and club trends across urban hits.


* **Chart 2: The Motswako Express Top 10**
* **Sponsor:** Cresta Hotels / Local Youth Brands
* **Number of Songs:** 10
* **Day of Show:** Thursday evenings
* **Presenter(s):** Resego Motlhokathari / DJ Izzy
* **Information:** Specialized chart tracking Motswako Hip Hop (Tswana-lyric hip hop) from both Botswana and South African trailblazers.


* **Chart 3: Yarona FM Top 10 Dance & House Chart**
* **Sponsor:** Castle Lite
* **Number of Songs:** 10
* **Day of Show:** Friday mid-mornings
* **Presenter(s):** DJ Fresh / Guest Resident DJs
* **Information:** Highlights high-energy Amapiano, Afro-house, and club tracks dominating Gaborone's nightlife and street culture.

* **Number of Songs:** 10
