How TrackHype works

Voting & Chart Rules

One ballot, fixed points, published at the Monday close. Here is exactly how ranked voting and chart tiers work.

The ballot

  • Every voter ranks up to 10 songs on one unified ballot: On Top, Contenders and Newest appear together under tier headings.
  • Points are fixed and always the same. 1st choice gets 10 points, 2nd gets 9, and so on down to 10th choice getting 1 point. Unranked songs get 0.
  • Only your most recent ballot counts. You can open your ballot and change your picks any time before the week closes — your new ballot replaces the old one.
  • A song can appear once per ballot, and you can only rank songs that are actually on the chart that week.
  • A chart with no competing songs cannot be voted on yet.

The chart week

  • Each chart week starts on Monday 00:00 and closes the following Monday 00:00 (Africa/Harare).
  • During the week you can vote and edit your ballot. Standings are not live — published rankings appear only after the close.
  • At the close the chart is finalised: On Top, Contenders and Newest are recomputed and an immutable snapshot is published for the week.

On Top

  • The official chart. Songs are ranked by total ballot points across On Top and Contenders ballots.
  • Chart size is fixed per chart (e.g. National Top 100 is 100 positions, genre charts are 20).
  • Movement is measured against the previous published snapshot: climbed, dropped, steady, or a NEW entry.

Contenders

  • 10 slots that bridge On Top and New Music, split in two: half go to the highest-scoring songs that fell out of On Top, and half go to the highest-scoring eligible Newest songs. If one side is short, the remaining slots spill over to the other side.
  • Contenders get one chance — any contender that does not reach On Top at the next close exits the contenders slot.
  • Contender ballots count exactly like On Top ballots toward the chart.

Newest

  • A one-month discovery lane for approved releases only — each new song gets four chart cycles before promotion or retirement. Songs enter Newest when moderation approves an artist submission — the submit form itself never places a song on a chart.
  • Newest ballots use the same 10-point scale, but points decide Newest promotion only — a song cannot jump straight into On Top from Newest.
  • After the one-month window (four chart cycles), a promoted song moves to Contenders and a non-promoted song retires from the lane (it can stay on New Music).

Tie-breakers

  • When total points are equal, the higher-ranked song is the one with more voters.
  • Still tied, the song that ranked higher in the previous published chart wins.
  • Next, the song approved earlier; finally the submission with the earlier TrackHype ID.

Artist submissions

  • Submitting music is a $10.00 USD per song payment flow and creates a submission record. It does not guarantee chart placement.
  • A submission must be approved before it appears in New Music / the Newest lane.
